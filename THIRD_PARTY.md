## Historical Model-Supply Data

The experiments use CSV snapshots from
[MSC-empirical/msc-empirical](https://github.com/MSC-empirical/msc-empirical)
at revision `0e719b8587e30f464f2e204127e844833efbe965`, associated with
*A First Look at Model Supply Chain: From the Risk Perspective* (ICSE 2026).
That pinned revision had no repository-level license when checked on
2026-07-16. Public access is not treated as permission to redistribute.

No upstream CSVs, repository-identifying instances, selected repository
lists, or model weights are included. The builder obtains historical inputs
from pinned upstream URLs and validates their recorded source metadata.
Access and use remain subject to the upstream terms and applicable permissions.

The package contains derived numeric aggregates and proofs represented by
candidate indices. Owner names in aggregate structural summaries are redacted.
